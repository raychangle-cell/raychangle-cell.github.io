FAQ

Common questions

Is Mediara AI a large language model?

No. It builds on top of existing foundation models. The core contribution is the multi-agent orchestration system — MultimediaExpert-Squad — that divides production across specialised agents. No neural network weights are trained from scratch.

What stage is it at?

Prototype. Core functionality works end to end and is being tested and refined. It is not yet deployed for production use, so no production performance figures are published.

Why a multi-agent design?

A single prompt rarely produces good video, copy and a campaign plan at once. Splitting the work lets each stage be tuned on its own, and makes failures easier to isolate and fix.
