Guidelines

How we keep it useful

Show the work

When sharing output, include the brief or input. Without it, results are hard to learn from.

Prototype honesty

Mediara AI is in testing. Share what actually happened, including failures — that is the useful part.

Accessible by default

Add alt text and captions where relevant. It is a community about making things usable for everyone.
